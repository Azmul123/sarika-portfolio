About Me

Hello, I'm Sarika Mandal. I am a first-semester B.Tech student in Computer Science and Technology at Narula Institute of Technology. I am deeply drawn to artificial intelligence and modern web development, focused on turning my academic learnings into practical, impactful digital solutions.

Beyond the screen, I am a passionate artist and musician. I believe that the harmony found in elegant code is very much like the melodies I create. As a singer and a multi-instrumentalist who loves playing the guitar, violin, and harmonium, I thrive in the creative spaces where technology and art intersect.

This unique blend of analytical problem-solving and boundless artistic expression defines who I am. Whether I am writing a new algorithm, designing a web interface, or plucking the strings of my guitar, I pour my creativity into everything I do.
